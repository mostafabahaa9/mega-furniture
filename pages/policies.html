Privacy Policy

How we collect and use information to serve you.

Information we collect

When you place an order, contact us on WhatsApp, or use our contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and order details.

Meta Pixel & advertising analytics

We use the Meta Pixel and Conversions API to measure site activity and improve advertising. This may include PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, Purchase and Contact events.

How we use your information

We use information to process orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support and improve our marketing. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

When information is shared

We share necessary data only with service providers who help us operate our business, such as payment processing, delivery, hosting, and customer data management. When the automated assistant is running, necessary parts of the conversation may be processed by AI and automation service providers to provide responses and follow up on the service. Access to data is limited to individuals and entities that need it for these purposes. We do not sell your personal data.

Facebook, Instagram messages & Automated Assistant

When communicating with Mega Furniture pages and accounts on Facebook or Instagram, we process the content of the messages and attachments you send, your platform account ID, and any name or profile data provided by the platform. We may retain data you provide during the conversation, such as your phone number, items of interest, and order details.

We use this data to respond to your inquiries, provide product information, follow up on your orders, and prepare contact details for the sales team. We may use an AI-powered automated assistant to understand messages and draft responses, with the ability to transfer the conversation to one of our staff when needed. You can request to speak to a staff member during the conversation.

Conversation Retention & Data Use

We may keep a record or summary of the conversation and the follow-up data needed to resume your service when you contact us again. We retain this data for the time necessary for customer service purposes, order tracking, and relevant legal obligations, then we delete it or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

Data Deletion Request

You can request the deletion of the conversation and follow-up data we retain by sending an email to:
megastorefurniture@gmail.com
with the subject "Data Deletion Request — Facebook / Instagram", specifying the platform and the account name you contacted us from. We may request limited information to verify that you are the requester; do not send your password or login codes.

We will review the request and clarify its completion or any data that must be retained and the reason for that. Deleting data from our systems does not automatically delete messages or data retained by Meta, which are subject to their settings and policies.

Contract, Cancellation & Warranty

Our commitment to clear conditions and product quality.

Contract and Cancellation Conditions

  1. Down Payment & Balance Settlement: A 30% deposit of the total contract value is required upon order confirmation, with the remaining balance due 6 days prior to delivery.
  2. Price Validity: Stated prices and quotation terms remain valid for 30 days from the contract date, after which agreements are subject to renewal.
  3. Custom & Tailored Orders: Custom-tailored furniture reservations are held for a maximum of 30 days, with full payment required before manufacturing begins.
  4. Custom Outdoor Furniture: Special-order outdoor pieces require full payment upfront to initiate production, with reservations held up to 30 days.
  5. Delivery & Assembly: Complimentary delivery and expert assembly are included for your initial delivery. Subsequent disassembly, relocation, or reassembly requests incur standard service fees.
  6. VIP Express Delivery: Orders requested for expedited delivery within 24 hours incur an optional premium service charge equal to 10% of the contract total.
  7. Exchange & Return Policy (Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018):
    • Returns and exchanges are permitted within 14 days of receipt provided items are unused, intact, and in their original packaging.
    • Replacements or returns are permitted within 30 days if an authenticated manufacturing defect is identified.
  8. Order Cancellation: Free cancellation with a full refund is available within 14 days of signing the agreement. After 14 days, a 10% administrative deduction applies. For returns post-delivery (in original factory condition), applicable shipping costs will be deducted.
  9. Refund Processing: Approved refunds are issued starting 48 hours after request confirmation, through the original method of payment (cash or card) and exclusively via the branch where the order was placed.
  10. Bespoke Built-to-Order Pieces: Furniture produced to custom client specifications cannot be exchanged or returned. In the event of an unavoidable cancellation, 25% of the item price is retained to cover fabrication and material expenses.
Important Notice: Contracts and purchase agreements expire 1 year from the date of issue. No refund claims or product collections can be honored after this period.

Warranty Conditions

  • The warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
  • Warranty Period (starts from the date of receipt):
    • 5 Years: Bedrooms and dining rooms.
    • 3 Years: Sofas, sectionals, salons, recliners, sofa beds, shoe cabinets, tables, and TV units.
    • 1 Year: Outdoor furniture and lighting.
  • If the product is dismantled, packaged, and installed without the company's knowledge, the warranty is considered void.
  • The warranty does not cover leather, fabric, glass, marble, LEDs in bedrooms and dining rooms, and outdoor cushion sets (please follow usage instructions).
  • The warranty does not apply in cases of misuse.
  • The warranty does not apply to commercial facilities, offices, and clinics.
  • The product cannot be returned unless it is in its original delivery condition (factory packaging).
  • The warranty does not cover accessories and handles.
  • The warranty does not cover all types of fabric and leather.
